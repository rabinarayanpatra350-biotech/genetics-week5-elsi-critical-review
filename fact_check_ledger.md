## How the sources were selected

Priority was given to (i) peer-reviewed primary research, (ii) the authoritative instrument or record for a claim
(a court judgment, a regulatory approval, a government programme's own guidelines, an official press release), and
(iii) contemporaneous reporting from established outlets for events where no primary document is public (for
example the details of a criminal sentencing). Contested empirical questions — principally the true prevalence of
genetic discrimination — are reported with the disagreement intact rather than resolved by selection.
